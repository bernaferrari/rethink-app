Keepout is a no-root firewall, encrypted DNS and VPN for Android. Decide which apps reach the internet, stop ads and trackers before they connect, and see every connection your phone makes.

FIREWALL
• Block any app on Wi-Fi, mobile data or both, with one tap.
• Isolate an app, let it bypass the rules, or exclude it from the tunnel.
• Universal rules: block apps in the background, when the screen is off, or when a new app is installed.
• IP, port and domain rules for one app or for every app.

ENCRYPTED DNS
• Rethink DNS with on-device blocklists for ads, trackers and malware.
• Or choose your own resolver over DNS-over-HTTPS, DNS-over-TLS, DNSCrypt or ODoH.
• DNS answers are checked locally, which also gets around DNS-based censorship.

SEE WHAT YOUR APPS DO
• Network and DNS logs: which app, which server, which country, allowed or blocked.
• Statistics for the last hour, day or week: top apps, blocked apps, countries, networks and domains.

VPN AND PROXIES
• Route apps through WireGuard, SOCKS5 or HTTP proxies, with split tunneling per app.
• One-tap Tor through Orbot.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Filtering runs on your device through Android's VPN service. Logs stay on the phone, you can turn them off, and there are no ads.

Keepout is an open-source fork of Rethink DNS + Firewall by Celzero, redesigned with Material 3 Expressive. Licensed under Apache 2.0.
